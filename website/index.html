Simple & Safe

The 2-Step Primary Workflow

StoMount was built from the ground up to make complex low-level ADB operations effortless, safe, and transparent.

STEP 01

Mount SD Card as Internal Storage

Connect your phone with USB Debugging enabled. StoMount scans your physical card (e.g., disk:179,64). Once you confirm with strict two-factor safety validation, StoMount formats the card into an adopted, hardware-encrypted private partition.

adb shell sm partition disk:179,64 private
STEP 02

Move All Apps & Content to SD Card

With the adopted storage volume mounted and verified, click Step 2. StoMount discovers every installed 3rd-party application and moves them one by one to your SD card, liberating gigabytes of precious phone memory in minutes.

adb shell pm move-package <package> <uuid>
Hardware Matrix

Manufacturer & OEM Compatibility

Learn how different phone manufacturers handle adoptable storage and how to get maximum performance.

Motorola / Nokia / Sony 100% Native

Near-stock Android builds have complete native support for Adoptable Storage. StoMount completes Step 1 & Step 2 automatically with zero extra configuration required.

Pro Tip: Use a Class 10 / UHS-1 (A1 or A2 rated) MicroSD card for maximum app launch speeds.
Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO Requires 1 Toggle

MIUI and HyperOS disable adopted menus by default. Enabling Developer Options and turning on "Force allow apps on external" provides full app migration capability.

Setting: Settings > Additional Settings > Developer Options > "Force allow apps on external storage" = ON.
Samsung Galaxy (One UI) App-Specific Guide

Samsung One UI kernel disables File-Based Encryption (fscrypt) on external microSD partitions. Attempting adopted private storage may yield Failure [-6].

Best Method for Samsung: Format SD as Standard Portable, enable "Force allow on external", then move apps via Settings > Apps > Storage > Change, or configure apps like Spotify directly to save offline media to SD!

Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about adopted storage, safety, or compatibility? Find answers here.

Adopted Storage (introduced in Android 6.0) allows Android to format a removable microSD card as an encrypted extension of the phone's internal storage. Instead of storing only photos and music, the card becomes capable of storing installed apps, private application data, games, and system downloads directly.

No, root is NOT required! StoMount operates entirely through standard Android Debug Bridge (ADB) permissions with developer mode enabled. Your phone's warranty and safety remain intact.

Because adopted storage is encrypted with hardware-tied keys and treated as part of internal memory, you should keep the card inserted. If removed, any apps stored on the SD card will temporarily be inaccessible until the card is plugged back in.

Samsung intentionally stripped Adoptable Storage support from One UI and blocks file-based encryption (fscrypt) on external microSD cards at the kernel level. For Samsung devices, we recommend formatting the SD card as standard portable storage, turning on "Force allow apps on external" in Developer Options, and moving individual app storage or media (such as Spotify offline music) directly.
